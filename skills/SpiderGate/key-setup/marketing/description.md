Get a workspace from "no key yet" to "my agent is calling models" without leaving the chat. The skill reads where setup stands and says the one next step with its link, saves the owner's answers, explains how to get a key for any provider (what it unlocks, its free limits, whether sign-up needs a phone check or a card), and saves a free key the owner pastes. The vault checks every key with its provider before saving, and the skill never repeats a key back. Paid keys and browser sign-ins (ChatGPT, Gemini) are never taken in chat: the owner gets the dashboard link instead. A teammate who holds an account can be proposed for an invite, which the owner sends. When a key is in, the skill tells the owner how to point OpenClaw, Hermes, n8n or Cursor at the gateway, and which agents cannot connect.
